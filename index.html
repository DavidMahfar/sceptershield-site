How we work

  1. Quote

    You receive a clear line-item quote with price, lead time, and country of origin.

  2. Source

    We source new, genuine products through manufacturers and authorized distribution channels.

  3. Verify

    Before quoting, we check each item against the solicitation's requirements, including Buy American, Trade Agreements, and Section 889 restrictions.

  4. Deliver

    We track each order through delivery and invoice through the system your agency requires.
